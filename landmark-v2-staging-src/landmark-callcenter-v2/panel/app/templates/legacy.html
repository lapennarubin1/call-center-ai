Switching the mode does not start or stop any workflow by itself. It records which system is in charge and refuses to change while the other one could still be calling — verified against n8n, not taken on trust. Turning workflows on and off stays where it always was, in Call Center.

While V2_PRIMARY is selected, a legacy group that dispatches, follows up, opens accounts, takes payments or processes call results cannot be turned on — not manually, not by Turn ON all, and not by its schedule. The attempt is refused and recorded. While LEGACY_BACKUP is selected, V2 routes return zero callable routes to WF2, even if their country, provider and route are all enabled.

Legacy groups

Not every legacy workflow conflicts with V2. A job that only mirrors CRM data into MySQL is idempotent and can keep running; one that places calls cannot. A group that has not been classified is treated as conflicting.
